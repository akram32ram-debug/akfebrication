About AK Fabrication

Strong. Reliable.
Built to Last.

AK Fabrication is a professional iron and steel fabrication service provider, trusted for strong materials, precise workmanship and clean finishing on every project.

From residential main gates and staircase railings to large industrial structures, every job is measured, engineered and welded with the same standard: fabrication that holds up — structurally and visually — for years to come. We work closely with each customer to translate a design idea, a rough sketch, or a site measurement into a finished steel product built exactly to requirement.

No two projects are identical, and we treat them that way — custom-fabricating gates, grills, frames and structures to fit the exact space, load and style our customers need.
